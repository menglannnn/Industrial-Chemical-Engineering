thermo: the outer phase check keeps the populated phases and adds the missing ones (D25)

Physics. A vessel's phases change by what happens to them: a phase is used up (boiled away, drained,
vented, dissolved), or it appears where the state can hold it, as a nucleus that then grows. Where T
and P leave the split open (a pure fluid on its saturation line, a node on a phase boundary) volume
and energy fix it, not a TP answer. The outer check took the TP answer at a pass's (T, P) as the
node's phase set and swapped one populated set for another: hold-fuzz seed 116's node of 1 kg of
water boiling under vacuum (304.2 K, 4.52 kPa) flipped between all liquid and all steam until the
active-set cycle refused the step, and a rigid liquid-full mixture vessel crossing its bubble point
was seeded with the TP split at an overshot (T, P), several vessel volumes of vapour it cannot hold.
Owner decision D25 (2026-10-15): "should not be limited to pure compounds for the sake of
future-proofing".

The rule (FluidThermodynamics.phaseCheck; no pure-compound test anywhere):
- a populated phase stays; it leaves only when the solve empties it, through the trace fold (D34,
  below 1e-9 of the node), also of an unsuccessful pass's last iterate;
- a phase the node's TP answer holds and the node lacks enters as a nucleus of 1e-6 of the node's
  amount (PHASE_NUCLEUS_FRACTION; the TP answer's own amount when less) at the TP composition,
  taken per component from the populated phases, so the totals stay the node's; the rows then grow
  it to the node's own split or empty it;
- a complete node (both hydrocarbon phases or neither, both water phases or neither) has nothing to
  add and is not flashed, whatever its vapour slot's root;
- a phase the trace rule removed in this solve is not added back (the foldedPhases record, A85).
PassiveStepSolver.phaseCorrection (still one node per call) and InventoryEquilibrium's regime loop
both call it; phaseCode reads FluidThermodynamics.phaseBits. Absorbed: main's pure-water TP flip,
the branch's pure-coexistence check and phaseCorrection's converged/unconverged switch.

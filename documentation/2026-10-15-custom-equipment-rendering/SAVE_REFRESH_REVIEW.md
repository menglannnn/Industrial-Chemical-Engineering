# Old-save equipment refresh and cyclone inlet: review (2026-10-18)
Branch `claude/custom-equipment-rendering-805e7d` on 1ca05c1c. Owner request: "Add a compatibility system so when loading an old save updated model is replaced by new ones" (an exception to the AGENTS.md no-compatibility rule, recorded there, equipment layouts only). Owner report the same day: "cyclone inlet looks weird".
## Real-life expectation first
- **Refit in place.** An upgraded design of an installed unit goes onto the same foundation and tie-ins: its body stays where it stood, the pipes stay connected at their sizes, its contents and paint stay, nothing beside it is demolished.
- **Cyclone inlet.** A rectangular inlet fed from a round pipe on another centreline is one fabricated eccentric transition, its walls within about 30° of the axis. Before: a square transition, then a two-bend dog-leg pipe to reach the nozzle's cell centre (the S the owner saw).
## What the code did, and does now
- **Before.** A cell's drawing always followed the code (its key is recomputed at load), but its set of cells did not: cells a new layout added were missing, cells it dropped stood as invisible collision, and a cell outside a shrunken box was rejected as invalid data.
- **Stamp.** `ShapeRasterizer.stamp(shape)`: an FNV hash of the box, the shell and interior cells and the ports, cached per geometry, equal on a dedicated server. Each shell cell saves it (`Layout`) with the box it was placed by (`Box`); a cell saved before today has neither and counts as stale.
- **Queue.** On server load a stale cell calls `EquipmentStructure.refreshLater`. The level tick takes at most one vessel whose whole region (old and new box, plus 3 blocks along x and z) is loaded and block-ticking; on most ticks nothing (one map lookup). Block structure, not process work: the tick-loop rule holds.
- **Plan.** `EquipmentRefresh.plan(layout, world)`, pure and gated. The new layout stands where it covers the most of the vessel's old cells (a shift within 3 blocks along x and z, never in y): the body stays on its foundation whichever side of the box a redrawn part grew or shrank on. Then the cells to clear, the shell and interior cells to place, and the layout cells another block holds (left out, never overwritten).
- **Apply.** `EquipmentStructure.refresh`: shell cells are the vessel's by origin and shape; interior cells by the east/south walk to a shell (`shellReached`, split out of `find`). Clears and places with the `dismantling` guard, rebinds every shell cell with its own paint, syncs cells whose key changed, updates pipes beside every changed cell, then `reconnect` and `refreshNozzles`; its chunks wait for their light before they are sent (`ChunkMap.waitForLightBeforeSending`). One log line: `equipment_refresh kind= origin= shift= cleared= placed= blocked=`; a failure is logged and leaves the vessel as it stands.
- **Drums.** The registration is edited in place (ports, volume, level height; temperature, pressure, composition and solids kept), so contents stay. The ledger never moves a device: a drum whose node cell moved registers anew, empty, and the log says so. `register`, `unregister` and the refresh share `node`, `position` and `ports`.
- **Cyclone.** One cyclone's transition is eccentric: its round end on the feed nozzle's cell centre, at least √3 times the offset long; the dog-leg pipe and its solid are gone. Rows of 2 to 4 are unchanged.
## Gates
- Gradle, one run: equipment.*, DeterministicMathSourceTest, UnitTextTest, ColumnStructureTest; 73 tests, 0 failures. `EquipmentRefreshTest` (5): stamps distinct and equal on a dedicated server; a current vessel unchanged; an older layout restored (strays, missing cells, a shell cell turned interior); the body kept on its foundation when the box shrinks at the front (the 2.0 m cyclone); another block never overwritten.
- Cyclone sweep (`tools/new-units/probes/Sweep.java`): 363 shapes, walk, climb, pipesApart and quads all pass.
## In-game checks (bridge lane, copies of the owner's review world; `tools/new-units/in-game/`)
- **First** (`COMPAT_IN_GAME_REPORT.md`): 35 refresh lines, one per structure; only the two cyclones changed cells (26/24 and 118/115), blocked 0; both match a fresh cyclone block for block; no strays; the drum keeps its registration; a second load is silent; the log is clean. Two faults: (1) the 2.0 m cyclone moved 1 block north (the box's corner was kept, the new box is a row shorter at the front), fixed by the anchor; (2) faces beside emptied cells drew black until a reload: the refreshes ran while chunks were still being sent, a chunk sent before the server relit it kept stale light (the server sends light changes only to players at their view's edge), fixed by block-ticking regions and the light wait.
- **Recheck** (`COMPAT_RECHECK_REPORT.md`): all pass. Both cyclones `shift=0,1`; the 2.0 m one clears 3 cells and places none, and the 1.1 m one clears 2 and places none. The first check had shifted the 1.1 m body too, unnoticed. The 2.0 m cyclone matches a fresh one placed by the same command block for block ("Test passed, count: 4875") and the kit check's view pixel for pixel, except the redrawn feed end. There were no black faces at 7–65 s and after the tour (near-black pixels as on a fresh cyclone; 5,528 before). A second and a third load are silent; the log is clean; there was no "Can't keep up". Units outside the simulation distance (8) refresh when the player comes near (3 units, 2–3 min). A 1.1 m cyclone placed fresh by the same command now stands 1 block north of the kept body: the command centres the box, and its 5 → 4 rows round differently. The body staying put is the intended result.
- Load cost: about 10 s of server time for 35 old structures, once per old save (one "Can't keep up"); the large units take 0.4–1.4 s each even unchanged, probably rebuilding geometries the cache (64 shapes or 300k facets) dropped; not measured further.
## Limits (owner propositions)
1. A layout cell another block holds stays out of the vessel (logged as a warning); the alternative is to refuse the refresh and keep the old cells.
2. A drum whose node cell moves loses its contents (the ledger cannot move a device).
3. A saved shape the current code rejects (a range that narrowed) stays unbound, as before; it would need a nearest-valid-shape rule.
4. A part-built vessel (debug `rows`) is completed by its refresh; the constructor always builds whole.

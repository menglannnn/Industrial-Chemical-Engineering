7. Owner decisions, 2026-10-16

  1. Arrangement: the side structure; the air cooler stands on its own frame, 1 m beyond the structure's outer end.
  2. PA returns: moved to the structure side, above their draws.
  3. Pumps: a reflux pump and one per PA, at grade.
  4. Reboiler: kettle.
  5. Air cooler size: face 8.5 × the column's cross-section (about 120 kW/m² at a 35 K air rise); bays of 2 to 5 m by 3 to 12 m tubes, at most four; two fans per bay; bundle 2 m over the reflux drum's top.
  6. Ejector: upright in the down-leg.
  7. Constructor: a page of its own: Condenser (Water / Air), Ejector (Off / On), Reboiler (Off / Kettle).

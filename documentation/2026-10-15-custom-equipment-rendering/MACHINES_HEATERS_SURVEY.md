# Machines and heaters: visual survey (2026-10-17)

Batch `2026-10-15-custom-equipment-rendering`, step "machines and heaters" (`MACHINES_HEATERS_PLAN.md`). The owner
judged the first draft as "gross shape looks ok, but needs significant improvement". This survey (an opus research
subagent, vendor sheets and practice; [src] = vendor or standard, typ. = practice) lists what makes each unit
recognisable at 1 m blocks (16 px: 1 px = 62.5 mm; parts under 0.05 m are skipped unless they are signature parts).

## API 610 OH2 pump (D2 = impeller diameter)
- Volute: outer radius grows from about 0.55 D2 after the tongue to 0.85–0.9 D2 at the throat (40–60 %): clearly
  eccentric. The spiral runs about 330°; the throat turns up into the vertical centreline discharge, with a visible
  notch on the tongue side. Rim width 0.35–0.45 D2; front face dished to the eye. Casing/cover joint ring about
  1.05 casing diameter with 16–24 studs: the strongest detail after the nozzles.
- Discharge: cast, a rectangular throat of about 1.2 DN tapering to a round flange, 0.15–0.3 m over the casing crown.
  Suction: a stubby cast bell, 0.6–1.0 DNs long; the suction is one size up. Flanges Class 300 (thicker than 150).
- Centreline feet near the cover end on pedestals 0.25–0.6 m tall. **No foot under the bearing housing (API 610).**
- Bearing bracket 0.25–0.45 m long with a large window each side (seal gland shows); bearing housing with a boxy oil
  sump at the front and 8–15 fins at the rear; constant-level oiler (bulb about 70 × 130 mm) on the side, vent on top.
- Seal: Plan 11 line from the discharge neck to the gland; Plans 52/53 a seal pot 0.15–0.25 × 0.8–1.2 m on its own
  post beside the bracket (very visible).
- Spacer coupling (≥ 125 mm) under a half-round hood 0.3–0.5 m long, yellow/orange/brass.
- TEFC motor: 30 kW about 0.8 × 0.45 m, 200 kW 1.35 × 0.75 m; 20–40 axial fins, feet, fan cowl 0.15–0.2 of its
  length with a grille, terminal box on top, lifting eyes.
- Baseplate of channel 0.15–0.3 m deep with a raised drain rim and grout holes; plinth 0.15–0.3 m over paving,
  0.1–0.3 m wider, chamfered, a 25–50 mm grout line.

## API 618 balanced-opposed compressor
- Envelopes (Siemens/Dresser-Rand HHE-FB, BDC): width over both cylinders 5.8–9.8 m, frame 0.9–1.9 m high, pitch
  0.5–0.93 m per throw. Per throw from the frame: crosshead guide (0.5–0.9 m across, 3–4 strokes long, bolted top
  door), distance piece (smaller, flanged both ends, 1–2 windows a side with bolted covers; a mid ring on C/D types),
  cylinder (body 1.4–1.8 bore, up to 2–3 for small bores; stroke + 0.5–0.8 m long; heavy flange), head (bolted cover;
  often a clearance pocket 0.4–0.6 cylinder diameters, projecting 0.3–0.6 m, with a handwheel).
- Valve caps 0.25–0.45 m, 1–2 per end on top (suction) and underneath (discharge) at ±30–45° beside the nozzle:
  4 on small cylinders, 8 on large. Suction nozzle top centre, discharge bottom centre.
- Outboard cylinder support under the head end. Bottles 1.0–1.6 bores across, 2.5–5 diameters long, 2:1 heads;
  suction bottle 0.2–0.5 m over the cylinder, discharge bottle 0.2–0.5 m under it on two stands. A PSV on each
  discharge line (0.5–1 m tall).
- Frame: ribbed walls, rectangular bolted covers on top (one per throw), breathers, oil pump on the free end.
- Driver: synchronous motor, a box (not a drum) 1.5–2.5 frame heights tall and as wide, 0.5–1 diameter long, with a
  cooler or top-hat hood, an outboard pedestal bearing and the exciter, a side terminal box. No flywheel with a
  synchronous drive (rotor inertia). Operating deck at frame-base level on large machines.

## API 560 fired heaters
- Floor 2.1–2.7 m or more over grade so an operator works under the burners. A natural-draft burner hangs as a
  muffler drum (0.8–1.2 m × 0.6–1.0 m) under an air register drum (0.5–0.9 m) with a gas riser from a ring or row
  header 1–1.5 m over grade.
- Peep doors (0.25 × 0.3 m) about 1.4–1.5 m over each platform; access doors 600 × 600 (cabin), explosion doors
  optional.
- Platforms (API 560 12.4.1): at burners not reachable from grade, both ends of the convection section, dampers and
  soot blowers, every observation port and access door. Rails 1.07 m with mid rail and toe board; caged ladders,
  cage from 2.1–2.4 m, flights offset between platforms.
- Casing: external columns over the legs and bands every 1.2–2.5 m, aluminium heat-resistant paint (columns the same
  colour); stack black or aluminium with a damper, actuator and lever near its base.
- Header boxes 0.6–1.0 m deep, faced with 2–4 bolted panels with handles. Soot blowers on one side of the convection
  section every 0.8–1.0 m, stub gearboxes about 0.5 m out.
- VC: 6 legs at D 3 m up to 12–16 at D 10 m (2–2.6 m apart), X-bracing in alternate bays leaving burner bays open;
  burner circle 0.35–0.5 D; 2–8 insulated crossovers from a convection header box down to the radiant inlet ring on
  the roof; hearth platform at the floor (360°), 270° platforms for further peep-door rows when the radiant section is
  over 8 m, a roof platform round the convection base.
- Cabin: two rows of columns 3–6 m apart, X-bracing in the end bays and every third or fourth bay, burner aisles open;
  two burner rows about 0.3 W in from the walls; hearth walkways the full length on both sides, convection-level
  walkways for the soot blowers, end header boxes like tall cupboards.

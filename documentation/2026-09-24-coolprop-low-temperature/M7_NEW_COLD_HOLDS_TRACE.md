# M7 new cold holds: seeds 30 (S1) and 19 (D36) minimized and traced (2026-10-17)

Batch `2026-09-24-coolprop-low-temperature`, branch `claude/unified-thermo-port` at `65036f2b`; diagnosis only, nothing committed (cold package fuzz, 4 threads, trace hooks applied per commit and reverted). The plain minimizer kept each class but lost the cause: its 10-block worlds also hold at the clean parents (30 at S4 stalls at 1.13e-9, logs 02-03; 19 is E7 at D34, log 09). Each world was therefore minimized on two commits at once, held at one and clean at the other: 30 from 13 to 11 blocks (E5 at `65036f2b`, clean at `22b76697`), 19 from 27 to 14 (E7 at `03bc70f6`, clean at `4199dbe6`). Neither hold depends on the budget: both fail on Newton verdicts at every step size.

## Seed 30 (S1, E5): a junction's vapour that must vanish, judged by a 1e-9 test at a failed iterate

**Physics.** The world is flat (z = 0) except the LNG generator (6 MPa, 169.7 K) at z = 1. The generator pours 71.98 kg/s down its 0.05 m line, at the line's velocity cap, into junction n5. From n5, 70.0 kg/s goes at its cap to a 1 atm void, and about 2 kg/s goes through 0.01 m lines via junction n2 to a 1 m3 tank (139 K, 577 kPa, 1.62 kg of CO2 frost). At tick 1593 a 0.05 m pipe joins a CO2-gas generator (2.03 MPa, 292 K, 92.6 % CO2) to the tank and n2. Expected over the 0.05 s interval: 7.8 kg/s of gas pours into the tank and the frost grows to 2.13 kg. The generator's line into n5 closes, because its flow would enter the generator. n5 goes back to 3.49 MPa at 169.8 K, all liquid, 1.3 MPa above the 2.18 MPa at which n2 boils, and n2 keeps 1.5e-4 of its mass as vapour. The interval start instead states n5 flashed at the generator's 2.03 MPa, with 4.5 % of its 56 mol as vapour, although it was liquid at 3.97 MPa before the event. The solve must remove that vapour.

**What the code did (log 05).**
- The rate solve converges, closes that line (16.9 kg/s into the generator), then fails its next pass on a line-search stall (|r| = 9.63, the void line's row). At that failed iterate n5's vapour is 1.0e-8 of its moles, above the 1e-9 trace fold, so the failed-iterate check (D28 A′) keeps it. All three rate tries end the same way.
- The substeps start without a seed. n5's vapour leaves in the 0.025 s attempt, and 0.0455 s of the 0.05 s is accepted. n2's vapour then shrinks toward nothing at its bubble point, ending at 3.5e-9 m3 (1.3e-7 of its mass) at 169.611 K and 2.1816 MPa. From 1.2e-5 s on, every failure is on n2's component rows. Their residual shrinks with the step to a floor of |r| = 1.474e-9 against the 1e-9 tolerance, and the steps go down to 3.6e-16 s: 68 failed Newtons and 405 k checks.

**Mechanism (logs 05, 06).** The two runs part at the rate solve's first failed iterate. At S4 that Newton stops on its iteration limit (|r| = 9.51) with n5's vapour at 3.6e-10, so the vapour is folded. The next pass converges (n5 at 3.495 MPa, liquid), and the interval is one 0.05 s step of 22 k checks. A populated phase leaves only below 1e-9 of its node's moles (D25), and the 1e-6 vanishing test of the D26 amendment exempts junctions. The verdict therefore depends on where a failed Newton happened to stop.

**Hypotheses.**
- (c) Confirmed. S1 removes the 9.81 J/kg of the 1 m fall, so from tick 1283 the junction runs 0.0028 K colder and 1.36 kPa lower (169.1109 K / 3 866 781 Pa, against 169.1137 K / 3 868 136 Pa). That is 2.8e-5 of the LNG's latent heat (about 350 kJ/kg), yet it moved the failed iterate's vapour 28-fold across the fold: the margin is nil. The fragility predates S1, since the 10-block world holds at both commits.
- (d) Refuted. `eec02758` drops g·z at every energy site, at both ends of each stream, and at HEAD GRAVITY is read only by static heads and settling. The failing rows are a hydraulic row and n2's component rows, never an energy row (n5J enthalpy |r| ≤ 1.9e-3). The two conservative-reconstruction refusals come at 3.7e-10 s steps, after the stall.

**Proposition (owner to choose).**
- **A.** Accept S1 with its four new holds (6, 30, 48, 161).
- **B.** Revert S1. g·z returns to five energy files (60 lines), and the junction buffers' exception returns with it. Seed 30 would be clean by luck (S4's iterate sat 2.8x under the fold), and the fragility stays.
- **C (recommended).** One vanishing rule for every node. A phase holding less than VANISHING_SHARE (1e-6) of its node's moles, and less than at the interval start, leaves at a converged pass or a failed iterate, whether the node is a junction or a vessel and whether the phase is populated or proposed. This is the D26 amendment's test applied to the solve's own iterate.
  - It deletes the junction exemption `junction?0:`. The 1e-9 fold stays for phases that do not shrink.
  - Here it removes n5's 1.0e-8 (from 4.5 %), which is S4's path, and n2's 1.3e-7 (from 13.8 %). 1e-6 of a junction is 5.6e-5 mol, about 1 mg of LNG, and the fold conserves every component.
  - Risk: a phase that a far-off failed iterate shrinks leaves early and must come back through D26's check. The D25/D26 gates and junction-trace references move. Unmeasured; seed 48 is likely the same class (its vapour at 1e-9 to 3e-9 motivated the 1e-6 share), but it was not traced.

## Seed 19 (D36, E7): a blow-down whose velocity-cap layout is found by chance

**Physics.** Before tick 2824, a CO2-rich gas generator (5.41 MPa, 300 K, 81 % CO2, 15 % N2, z = -1) and a 1 m3 tank at z = -2 (87.4 kg, 349 K) rest with a header of 0.05-0.1 m lines at z = -1, all at 5 409 675.94 Pa. Under D34 the tank stood 1150 Pa higher and held 18 g more. An LNG junction n4 (125.6 K, z = 0) hangs at 5.40 MPa on a 1 m riser (0.02 m) from the header. Its 0.01 m line from a 441 kPa, 127 K LNG generator carries nothing. At tick 2824 a 1 atm void opens on two 0.1 m header vents and on a 0.02 m line from n4. Expected (D34's first converged step): the generator line runs at its 23.0 kg/s cap and the tank line at 17.2 kg/s. The header falls to 1.35 MPa, with both vents at 19.5 kg/s, and the generator's junction n5 stands at 3.21 MPa. n4 flashes to 0.13-0.25 MPa at 124-128 K and passes 0.6-0.7 kg/s of LNG plus 1.3 kg/s of gas. Nine of the twelve lines sit at their caps, and the tank blows down with a time constant of about 5 s.
- (a) The gases weigh 117.3 kg/m3 at the generator (1150 Pa/m), 87.4 in the tank (857), 101 in the header (991) and 50.8 at 2.68 MPa (498). That is 20 to 100 times D36's 11-58 Pa/m. Per pascal, however, it is 1.6-2.1e-4 per metre, against 1.1-1.2e-4 in D36's cases: a gas column is M·g/(Z·R·T) of its pressure, at any pressure. Near-critical LNG (seed 30's, 366.6 kg/m3) reads as liquid and keeps its column of 3.6 kPa.

**What the code did (log 12).**
- The rate solve fails, and so do all 20 substeps from 0.05 s down to 9.5e-8 s (215 passes, 202 failed Newtons, 355 k checks). Each first pass stalls at |r| ≈ 88 on the continuity rows of n0, n1, n3 and n5, starting from the interval start, where the topology event states n0 and n5 at 0.22-0.23 MPa between neighbours at 5.4 MPa.
- The active set then walks through about 10 layouts per attempt. In the 4.88e-5 s attempt it changes 11 times: 10 are velocity caps (edges 0, 1, 2, 3, 5, 9, 10, 11) and one is n4's flash (1→3).
- That flash is added at a failed iterate at 148 K and 1.3 Pa (45 iterates put n4 at 1-1.9 Pa). It is seeded at n4's PH answer there, a 52 K state of 11 054 m3/kg, so each later pass starts at |r| = 4.7e11 on n4's three lines. Each attempt ends on a failure that no correction changes, or on a repeated pass key (pass 9 at 9.5e-8 s).

**Mechanism (logs 12, 13).** D34 runs the same search (147 passes, 129 failed Newtons, 247 k checks). Its 11th attempt (4.88e-5 s) converges at passes 15 and 17, and the steps then grow back to 0.025 s. The two runs part at the rate solve's pass 6, from the same layout and the same 4.7e11 start. D36 stops at |r| = 172.7 on n4's vent and component rows, with the header at 2.26 MPa. D34 stops at 119.0 with the header at 1.21 MPa; its correction caps e5 and e9 (n5 to the header) and goes on, while D36's correction finds no new layout. The equations differ only in the gas heads D36 drops: e0 857 Pa, e4 and e8 498 Pa, e1 44 Pa, which is 1.6-1.9e-4 of the local pressure.
- (a) Confirmed per metre, refuted as the mechanism. The balance moves by at most 2e-4 of P; the hold is a re-draw of a chaotic search.
- (b) Refuted. Every gas node keeps phase code 2 in every pass of both runs. At every pass start its PIP (0.41-0.98) stays below max(1, Z), with Z at 0.81-0.99. Only failed iterates read liquid-like in the vapour slot: n0 at 89.6 MPa and 457 K (4 at D36, 2 at D34), and n4 at 3.3 MPa (2 at D34). The pass layout fixes the slot anyway. e1's column end follows the flow: LNG at 449.6 kg/m3 (4410 Pa), or n0's gas, which weighs 0 under D36 and 44 Pa under D34. That is the same 4.4 kPa jump in both.

**Proposition (owner to choose).**
- **A (recommended).** Keep D36 and accept the hold. It is one new hold, a re-draw of a search that D34 nearly lost as well (its 10-block world is E7). Seeds 85 (E7 to E5) and 110 (B to E5) only changed class. Nothing is deleted or added.
- **B.** Revert D36. Seed 19 becomes clean again, by the same chance. The 116.7 Pa riser drive, the 31.6 Pa gas thermosiphon and the gas-only junction's column scaling return, 15 runtime references move back, and the fragility stays.
- **C (structural, aimed at the fragile stage rather than D36).** Make the cap part of every line's law: q = q_law·(1+(q_law/q_cap)^8)^(-1/8), one smooth clamp in place of the cap active set.
  - It deletes CAP_OPEN/CAP_START, capTransition, correctCaps, startCaps, the capped-junction release and the caps in the pass key (56 lines of PassiveStepSolver). The flow is 8.3 % under the sharp clamp at the knee, and within 0.06 % beyond a factor 2 on either side.
  - Risk: large and unmeasured. Every capped reference moves (chain-100, MIXED_GAS_COST, the cap gates). It needs a plan and the 200-seed quick check.

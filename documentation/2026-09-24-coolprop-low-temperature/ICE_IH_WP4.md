# Ice Ih as a crystal, WP4: report (2026-10-16)

**Physics first.** Free water is pure water beside the hydrocarbons. Neither dissolves the other, and there are no hydrates (decision 6 A). Below its melting line, T_m(P) = 273.16 K - 7.42e-8 K/Pa (P - 611.657 Pa), its condensed phase is ice Ih: T_m is 273.1526 K at 1 bar and 272.418 K at 10 MPa. The steam over ice keeps IAPWS R14-08's sublimation pressure: 259.9 Pa at 263.15 K (a supercooled liquid would keep 286.5 Pa) and 5.6e-12 Pa at 111.6 K. Expected: nitrogen at 1 bar holding 1170 Pa of water (50 % humidity at 20 °C), cooled to -10 °C, keeps 260 Pa, so 0.91 % of its moles (78 % of its water) leave as ice. Water in LNG at 111.6 K all freezes: from 20 °C it gives up 12.0 kJ/mol (1.5 liquid, 6.0 fusion, 4.4 ice) and boils 1.46 mol of methane per mol. At fixed P, a PH or UV state inside the freeze step sits on T_m with the ice fraction its balance gives. Liquid water at 25 °C let down to 100 Pa boils until cold and then freezes: 252.8 K, 17 % steam, 83 % ice. Ice coexists with LNG, gas and CO2-I.

**What the code does now.**
- Data: `crystals/ice_ih.json` is a segments record: two c_p polynomials fitted to R10-06, anchored to IF97's liquid at 273.16 K and 611.657 Pa, with fusion 6007 J/mol and a linear volume of 19.65 cm3/mol. `water.json` is r2; its `ice` block names the record and carries R14-08, read by `MaterialCatalog` as `Water.Ice`. Ten `fluid_domain` evidence texts now state the data range, the notice and the ice. The physics fingerprint leaves water out, so the neural pin b1b9f1eb is kept; the package scientific revisions move.
- Engine: `FreeWaterIce` (new) gives T_m by Clapeyron, from the record and the water model's liquid (-7.4163e-8 K/Pa). It also gives the ice's fugacity f = p_sub(T) p_sat(T_m) / p_sub(T_m), which equals the liquid's on the line. In `FluidTpEquilibrium`, free water below T_m is ice with its steam at f. PH and UV answer the freeze step from the balance, as they answer the boiling step. Wet feeds compete crystals: `solveWetWithCrystals` keeps `solveWet` unless a crystal forms or it fails. Otherwise it solves the hydrocarbons dry at pc = P - p_w and lays the water around them, a fixed point on pc with error v p_w/RT (below 1e-7 beside ice). Deleted: the ICE hold, the water exclusion in `crystalsCompete`, and the wet-crystal refusal.
- Network (D23): `NetworkCrystals` adds the ice to every package, last, with D23's constants at the normal melting point (916.8 kg/m3, 2.097 kJ/(kg K), `crystal-ice-ih-r1@<pkg>`). TP answers and steps carry the ice as its stand-in, free liquid water held at its 273.16 K values. A vessel's refresh freezes the stand-in and the steam: `VesselCrystals` reads the ice against the steam, else the liquid, else all the water. The rule "free water holds no crystal" is gone, and `PassiveIntervalSolver` refreshes on every package. Sizes follow D23, from 0.25 mm frost to 1.01 mm at the 0.5 slush share. `PhaseLayout.LOG_READ_SPAN`: where a vessel carries liquid water and steam, the steam's difference step is capped at 1e4 times its seed, so a trace steam read through a log keeps a secant near its tangent. This is bitwise wherever the steam is at least 1e-8 of the vessel. `HydrocarbonModel`'s revision adds `separate-free-water-v2` and the water revision (fresh world).

## 1. The cold cost and classes E7/F are one mechanism: an own split that seeds a phase its own closure drove to nothing
- Physics. Seed 48: an LNG tank beside 68 kg of dry ice, liquid-full (liquid plus solids fill the capacity in every stalled closure, probe 06) at its bubble point. A boiling liquid in a vessel with no room does not boil: the pressure rises and holds it (D30's physics). Seed 183: a gas tank with 0.03 kg of frost at ambient pressure, its gas at the dew point: a drop that forms re-evaporates. In both the TP flash at the pass's point sits on a phase boundary and says "a phase", and the vessel at its own energy and volume says "none".
- Code. `phaseCheck` takes the TP flash as the proposal and sizes it by the vessel's own closure (D26, `InventoryEquilibrium.split`: 24 Newton iterations at 1e-11). The closure drives the proposed phase towards zero and stalls; `split` seeds its last iterate anyway ("it is only a seed", `InventoryEquilibrium.java` `split`); the fold (`PHASE_TRACE_FRACTION` 1e-9) then keeps or drops that phase at rounding, the pass key cycles, the interval halves to its limit. Probes (seed 48, alone, bitwise reproducible): 142 079 own splits (88 % of the campaign's), 141 989 at the iteration cap, 3.41 M of the seed's 4.01 M Newton iterations inside own splits called from the step's phase checks (converged points 2.28 M, failed iterates 1.11 M, polish 17 k); every one asked for a vapour in a vessel holding solids; 98 % of the stalled iterates hold a vapour of ~1e-9 of the moles (the fold's own threshold, 1e-8 of the capacity) or none, at residual ~1e-6, and 49 900 of 94 924 survived the fold into a pass. Seed 48 alone is 87 % of the campaign's Newton iterations and 35 % of its time; seeds 48, 118 and 183 are 63 %. The campaign's holds: 95 active-set cycles, 87 interval substep limits, 34 wall deadlines (E7, F); seed 183 adds 15 490 own splits, 4 029 converged, the stalls at residual ~1 with the liquid driven to 0 or 1e-11. The refresh closures cost 3.8 k iterations in seed 48; the stand-in asked in 5 of 142 079 proposals there, 6 387 of 15 490 in seed 183 (section 2).
- Rule-off probe: "an unconverged own closure adds nothing" takes seed 48 from 4.01 M to 1 991 iterations but holds it at the methane floor after two steps: 40 closures adding a real liquid to a gas tank stall as well. Non-convergence is not the verdict; the direction the closure took is.
- Proposal. **A (recommended).** The own split answers "no phase" when its closure, converged or not, has driven the proposed phase under the trace share, and `phaseCheck` records that refusal in the solve's A85 record (the same bits a fold writes), so the TP flash does not re-propose it in this solve; a closure whose phase stands seeds its iterate as now. One principle for every closure: D30 gave it to the refresh's joint closure (a bubble with no room leaves); this gives it to the step's own split. Deletes: the raw last-iterate seed of a vanishing phase, the fold's coin flip on it, and with them the E7/F churn; cost at most one closure per proposal and none on later passes of the solve. Risk: a phase that is legitimately tiny and growing (a bubble forming in a cooled full tank) is refused for this solve; it returns through the refresh (D30 `passes`) and the next interval. Judge: seeds 48 and 183 alone first (the storm either vanishes or not), then the 200-seed cold and bundled quick checks. **B.** Loosen the own split's tolerance (it is a seed: 1e-6, not 1e-11): cheaper stalls, same churn. **C.** As is.

## 2. Carrying a crystal through a step: keep the stand-in as the energy carrier, take away its vote
- Physics. Snow's energy is a liquid's (fusion 9 kJ/mol, sublimation 25); a supersaturated gas lasts seconds. Between refreshes the frost share must be booked condensed, and it must not be offered to the rows as a fluid phase they cannot hold.
- Code. `adopt` books the frost share in the liquid slot as a Peng-Robinson liquid of pure CO2 (`92d39275`); that liquid then votes in `phaseCheck` (it is "a liquid the node lacks", and a node whose slot it fills is "liquid-complete"), which cost D29 A (support widening) and D31 (the hijacked slot at arrival). In seed 183 the pseudo-liquid is 41 % of the proposals and every closure of it stalls: a pure-CO2 liquid cannot stand in a methane gas tank.
- Forms weighed. An inert carried solid at the record's properties needs crystal unknowns in the step's rows (the plan excluded them; rightly, they are the Joint machinery in every step). Frost as vapour (M2) puts 25 kJ/mol of deficit in the gas: the methane floors, measured. A fluid-only oracle (`tpCrystalsHeldOut`, which the network never calls) gives the pure let-down its metastable liquid but holds row E's second liquid for ever (the CO2 never arrives). So the stand-in stays, as the lumping of condensed matter it is.
- Proposal. **A (recommended).** The stand-in has no vote: `phaseCheck` counts a crystal-species-only liquid beside a vapour (`condensedShareOnly`) neither as a proposal nor towards liquid-completeness (D31 B, generalised to every node); D31 A (the empty bag laid out at its own split) stays as D26 applied at arrival and should be applied; D29 A stays as the support of the stand-in beside a real liquid. Deletes the stand-in's share of section 1 and the arrival special case's reason. Risk: none physical; the energy booking is unchanged. Judge with section 1's forms (one quick-check pair for A1+A2 together, the lean cap).

## 3. One principle for the phase set
"The TP flash proposes; a node's own closure decides, by where it goes; only the solve removes (a fold, D34; an emptying, D59; a carried gas, D41); every removal or refusal is recorded for the solve (A85); a failed iterate's fold is a trial that reads the record and never writes it (D28 A′); an empty bag's first layout is its own split of what arrives (D31 A); the bag wall is a row (D54)." D25, D26, D28 A′, D31 and the two proposals above are its instances. What it deletes: the stalled-iterate seed (1), the stand-in's vote (2), the TP flash at the mass-weighted temperature in `arrivingPhases` (D31 A). What it keeps: `tpSeed` as the fallback seed when the closure returns nothing (1 of 160 518), the pass-key cycle guard, and D28 A′ (its −21 % passes stand until the quick check under A says otherwise).

## 4. M4's frosting tank (open item 2): zero solid flux is a row, not a closure
- Physics. Gas leaves a gas-filled tank through any nozzle; the frost stays on the walls; a bed of dry solids is permeable to gas. A closure stands for its interval only.
- Code. A bulk end on a donor without liquid is a MIXED outlet; `SolidMobility.check` closes it both ways (NO_LIQUID_CARRIER, or PACKED_SLURRY at a solid share of 1) because `ConservativeTransport` books a bulk draw's solids as `solids.scale(moved/mass)`, which would carry frost out with gas (lines 453, 460, 490). `NetworkReduction.atRest` lifts closures at an interval start only while a node holds solids (`solids&&flows==null`, D23 (6)), so a closure outlives the frost that caused it (M4's 199 kPa island at rest).
- Proposal. **A (recommended).** Solids ride the liquid: one helper books a bulk draw's solids as `solids × liquid drawn / liquid held`, zero from a dry gas (the same number as today whenever liquid is present), and `SolidEventIntegrator.failed` reads a bulk end on a donor without liquid as the GAS outlet. Deletes `Reason.NO_LIQUID_CARRIER` and the closure for it; the owner's B falls out as a row. And the rest test reads every transport closure as open at an interval start, solids or not (drop the `solids` flag in `atRest`; `reopened` keeps a stopped filter's closure, `InlineFilter.stoppedAtCapacity`): deletes D23 (6)'s special case; M4's A is this half. Risk: frost stranded in a junction that lost its liquid stays there (a frosted pipe; it passes gas); a dry bed never discharges (D23 OD-P4 already says so). Judge: M4's in-game check (07b, 16) and the bundled quick check. **B.** M4's A alone (the stuck closure, not the shut gas).
- Open item 1 (islands of one saved without members, main 0.8.0 runtime) does not touch the port's design; M4's A (retire the boundary's island of one in `affected`; capture and load refuse a member-less island) is the structural form.

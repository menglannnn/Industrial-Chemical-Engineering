## H4, row 10: water frozen in a liquid-full methane tank
- **Physics.** Water at 298 K and 1 MPa entering liquid methane at 125 K freezes and gives up about 12 kJ/mol. That boils about 1.4 kg of methane per kg of water, which leaves with the through-flow. At the refresh, the tank (25,360 mol of CH4, 551 mol of water of which 4.3 kg is ice) is liquid methane and 9.9 kg of ice.
- **Code.** The refresh's crystal test (`VesselCrystals.settle`, then `crystalsAtVolume`) brackets the answer, liquid CH4 + ice at 124.7187 to 124.7199 K and about 0.33 MPa, then fails with "no convergence of the UV pressure search at 124.718 K in 100 TP equilibria" (`h4-uv-head.log`, M10 hook). At 124.6307 K the target volume lies in methane's own saturation step at 263.9 kPa: 0.9998 m³ on the liquid side, 93.6 m³ on the vapour side. `innerUv` answers such a step only for a dry pure feed, a crystal step, and the free water's boiling and freezing; a wet feed with one hydrocarbon has no case, so the search fails there. The secant it stored across that jump (8.8e11) starts every later temperature with a step of 1e-16: 100 TP equilibria at one pressure (328,199.83 Pa). The D21 guard against such secants keys on classification and phase count, which both sides of this jump share (FREE_WATER, two phases).
- **Side note (physics, owner to judge).** Below 273.16 K the stand-in water continues Region 1 with a cp of 76 J/mol/K, while the ice's cp falls to 19 J/mol/K. Fusion therefore releases −1.04 kJ/mol at 125 K: the refresh cools the tank by 0.6 K, and its pressure falls from 0.96 to 0.33 MPa. The total heat from 298 K water to 125 K ice stays right (12.1 kJ/mol). The comment in `FluidThermodynamics.state` says the stand-in is "held at its 273.16 K values"; the code continues it instead.
- **Commits.** It holds at tick 1675 at `87c106f6` ("lies in a step of V(P) at 124.669 K") and at every later commit. Port-only: at `c9e7c128` the row held earlier, typed as a water-domain hold, before WP4 and the rule that leaving a data range never holds.

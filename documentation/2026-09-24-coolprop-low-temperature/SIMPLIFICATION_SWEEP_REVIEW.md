Examined and rejected (one line each).
- Pump shaft work booked as heat into the discharge (D8; `ConservativeTransport` 490): it is the flow work `v Δp` that the discharge liquid's enthalpy at the higher pressure requires; without it a pumped liquid cools 0.07 K per 3 bar, an artefact, not a simplification.
- Compressor isothermal power (D8) → `q Δp`: at ratio 3, `ln 3 = 1.10` against 2, 45 % more discharge heat; owner decision; not small.
- Level head `g m_c H/V` at the bottom port (phase-ports D9): about 9.8 kPa in a full 1 m³ tank, 10 % of 1 atm.
- `PUMP_SUCTION_LIMIT` cavitation mode (phase-ports D59 (4), A390): nine campaign worlds hold without it.
- D6 inlet hysteresis bands (2 %/0.5 %, 1 %/0.2 %) → one threshold: phase-ports A16 measured the duty-cycling a single threshold gives.
- Ice melting-line slope (`FreeWaterIce`, Clapeyron −7.42e-8 K/Pa): 0.74 K at 10 MPa, but it is what bounds a rigid vessel's freeze (0.28 ice at 67 MPa, commit `703d69b3`); without it that pressure is unbounded.
- Ice fugacity scale `p_sat(T_m)/p_sub(T_m)` (1 + 7.3e-5 at 1 bar): removing it puts a 7e-5 step into the condensed test at the melting point, a new discontinuity.
- Volume translation in `ln φ` (`P c_i/RT`, D14): 2e-4 at 1 bar but 2 % at 10 MPa (0.5–1 K of bubble point), and the EOS volume and fugacity would disagree.
- CO2-I as a sublimation-curve crystal like the ice (deleting `JaegerSpanGibbsFunction`, 282 lines): D16/D17 chose the Gibbs function on holdout data and rejected the segment record for missing expansion, compressibility and cp above 189.78 K.
- Network ice cp held at its melting value (37.8 J/(mol K); 1.7 kJ/mol = 14 % too much latent heat at 111.6 K): already the simplest form; the open item (MAIN_ADAPTATION_REVIEW) is accuracy, not simplification.
- `LiquidMixtureCorrection` (symmetric pair groups): live in all eight crude packages with descriptors on pc01–pc12; deleting it changes crude liquid viscosity outright.
- Coefficient path (`shifted_polynomial_5`, `TranslatedPengRobinson`) → spine only: the coefficient path is the production path of every tjl20 package; the spine serves the pilot only.
- Bulk and vapour acoustic bounds (`isothermalAcousticBound`, `vaporVelocityLimit`): they bind for two-phase bulk streams (about 20 m/s for a half-gas water junction at 1 bar) and heavy vapours (98 m/s at M ≈ 300), and size the junction buffers (`m_J` reads `velocityLimit`); deleting them resizes every two-phase junction line.
- Laminar/turbulent friction blend → turbulent only: the turbulent law's slope at zero flow is zero (singular rest Jacobian; `resolutionFlow` reads the laminar slope).
- Junction holdup `HOLDUP_TAU 0.05 s` (D46), pinned flows `f_J = In'/Out` (A232), trace flush 1e-24 booked as a boundary (D16, A234): owner-kept regularisations of the ledger.
- Trace thresholds (`PHASE_TRACE_FRACTION 1e-9`, `VANISHING_SHARE 1e-6`, truncation 1e-6 with reinsertion ×10, `HOLDS_NOTHING 1e-12`, `TRACE_DIFFERENCE_GUARD`), `STATE_CAP 0.05`, `FIXED_POINT_ROUNDS 1`, the small-headspace 1e-10 tier, the D13 polish, the A414 chord rule, `BOTH_DIRECTIONS` closures, `shutoffBand`: numerical, each with a measured reason in its comment.
- `SPARSE_PAIRS` kernel → classical, the private `pureLiquidCompressibility` copy, `SLUSH_SHARE`/`SLUSH_FRACTION` 0.5 twice, `AMBIENT_PRESSURE` beside `NetworkReduction.ambient`: consolidation only (the first two move chain-100 bits), no physics.
- One population per solid everywhere (64 populations; `oneSizePerSolid` only at refresh): the mobility check reads the largest population; not quantified here; the solids batch's design (D22/D23).
- Water notices (`waterVaporPressureLimit` 7-step table, `METASTABLE_MARGIN_PASCAL`): notices only under the owner rule of 2026-10-16, no physics to simplify.

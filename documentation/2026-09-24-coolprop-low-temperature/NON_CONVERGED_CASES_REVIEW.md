## K1: a let-down or vent line that chokes (51)
- **Physics (694).** 4.57 MPa, 161 K LNG (bubble point about 1.7 MPa) is let down through 2 m of 50 mm pipe to a 1 atm void, with a branch through a 200 kPa valve into an empty bag. A real line chokes: the subcooled critical flux is about √(2ρ(P0 − Psat)) ≈ 49 t/(m²·s), about 96 kg/s at 50 mm; the model's 100 m/s cap gives 77.5 kg/s. The jet flashes to about 34 % vapour by mass at 112 K and 1 atm (cp·ΔT/λ = 3.5 × 49 / 510). Steady: the vent on its cap, the junction two-phase at a few bar, and the bag filling at its bubble point. Gas (535): 3.7 MPa CO2-rich gas vents through 10 mm lines, which all choke.
- **Code.** No pass converges at any step from 0.05 s down to 9.5e-8 s (108 passes, 91 failed Newtons). The four line caps are redrawn through about 8 layouts per attempt, including caps whose sign contradicts their line's pressure difference (e2 carries flow from 158 kPa into 2.7 MPa). In 535 a junction's 3.7 MPa holdup empties within the step, and the cap of line n2-n3 flips sign on each pass. 589 and bundled 372 (E2, singular) are this group's capped-junction form (A341). H5 is the mover-mode form, a compressor between a slack bag and its ratio limit.
- **Verdict B.** A choked line is ordinary physics with one smooth answer.
- **Proposition.** M7 and M8 option C: the cap is part of every line's law, q = q_law (1 + (q_law/q_cap)^8)^(−1/8), odd in q_law, so its sign never needs a set. It deletes CAP_OPEN, CAP_START, capTransition, correctCaps, startCaps, the capped-junction release and the caps in the pass key (56 lines). The flow is 8.3 % under the sharp clamp at the knee and within 0.06 % beyond a factor 2. The same form would serve the mover limits (H5) later. Risk: every capped reference moves; it needs a plan and the 200-seed quick check.
## K2: warm CO2 gas into LNG near the critical region (18)
- **Physics (518).** 1.46 kg/s of LNG (164.8 K, 4.65 MPa) and 0.58 kg/s of gas (68 % CO2, 286.5 K) mix in a 3 L junction at 3.93 MPa and 187.5 K, then flow into a two-phase tank at 2.45 MPa holding 12 kg of CO2 frost. The engine's TP answer at the junction is two-phase: vapour 46 % of the volume, 17 % of the mass, densities 81 and 335 kg/m³. That is a real near-critical CH4-CO2 split, and its steady state is stationary.
- **Code.** Laid out two-phase, the Newton drives the vapour to 1e-157 and stalls at |r| 1-3e-3 on the junction's four fugacity rows. Both phases sit on one cubic root (roots=1), the trivial solution. The failure branch folds the vapour, the liquid pass converges, and the converged-point check proposes the vapour again from TP: a cycle at every step size (20 attempts). D39's refusal does not apply, because the vapour was populated at the interval start, not proposed.
- **Verdict B.** The check and the Newton disagree about the same state.
- **Proposition (owner to choose).** (a) Structural: the converged-point check judges a node with the closure the Newton solves (its own PH at its enthalpy, the Newton's root choice), so it proposes no phase the next pass cannot hold. (b) Lighter: a phase that the failure branch folded is not proposed again within the same step; D39's refusal record gains "folded" bits. Effect of (b): 518's junction stands liquid for that step, so 0.17 kg of vapour is misplaced in a 3 L holdup, conserved and corrected at the next step. Risk: a junction's column density moves from about 220 to 335 kg/m³ for that step, about 1 kPa on a 1 m line. Unmeasured.
## K3: a phase at a node's resolution (19)
- **Physics (1).** 6 MPa, 167 K LNG pours at 290.6 kg/s (100 mm cap) into an empty bag, which flashes to its 1 atm bubble point, 113.04 K. Its gas pushes 5.1 g/s into a second empty bag. Nothing else happens.
- **Code.** The second bag holds 7e-12 kg, laid out two-phase (1e-15 m³ of liquid). Its four fugacity rows stall at |r| = 7.17e-7 at every step, down to 2.8e-18 s (125 attempts). Laid out dry, it converges in 0 iterations, and the next step reopens it. M8's 48 (a bubble-point receiver at 3.4e-6) is the same group; D39 B cleared that seed. Bundled 431 (the first grams of hot crude) and the 1e-8 equation gates (cold 473, bundled 803) belong here. The remaining tails stall at |r| 4e-11 to 1e-4.
- **Verdict B.** A representational resolution limit, not physics.
- **Proposition.** One resolution rule in the layout: a phase holding less than an absolute resolution (VANISHING_SHARE times the node's full-volume content, about 0.4 g of LNG for a 1 m³ bag, rather than times its current moles) is not laid out. An arrival then stands in its own phase until the bag holds more than that. It absorbs A394 ("holding nothing at the bag's resolution"), rule C's share and D39 B's refusal into one test. Risk: the D25, D26, D38 and D39 gates and the junction trace references move.
## K4: a tank crossing to liquid-full (6)
- **Physics (504).** A slack crude bag at 1 atm (661 kg, 0.77 m³ of liquid) fills from a 319 kPa, 297 K source at about 53 kg/s, so its remaining 0.15 m³ fills in about 2.5 s. Its light-end vapour is compressed and redissolves, the pressure rises to 319 kPa and the flow stops: a liquid-full tank at rest.
- **Code.** Each step alternates between two layouts: two-phase at 116 kPa with 2.5e-4 m³ of vapour, or liquid-only and liquid-full at 215-302 kPa. The Backward-Euler state-change error stays at 0.08 at any step size. Steps fall to 1e-10 s, and 1024 attempts advance 3.65 of 5 s (E8). The cold seeds 206, 424 and 543 hold beside liquid-full tanks at 4-6 MPa; 543's tank is sealed by five blocked lines at 5.9 MPa, above every source. H4 (a liquid-full CH4 tank freezing 9.9 kg of ice, |r| 42) is inferred to belong here.
- **Verdict B.** This is the open stiff-liquid design item (phase-ports batch).
- **Proposition (owner to choose).** A taut bag with a compliance: V = V0 (1 + (P − 101325)/K_bag), K_bag about 10 MPa. At 319 kPa that is 22 L more (2.2 %), and the pressure rises over about 0.4 s of inflow instead of jumping. It deletes the jump at the slack-to-taut crossing; D40's PH-slack/UV-taut split becomes one law. Risk: every taut-tank reference moves (STACK_PASS, rig rows 9 and 10). Unmeasured.

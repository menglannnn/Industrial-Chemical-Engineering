## Proportions read off the photos, and what the model takes

| Item | Survey | Model (sheets approved 2026-10-21) |
|---|---|---|
| Tank H/D | 0.25–0.3 for big crude tanks; D 40–110 m, H 15–22 m; product tanks D 20–40 m [unc] | grid D 4–40 m, shell 4–24 m (unchanged) |
| Shell courses | 2.0–2.5 m plates (8 ft common) [unc] | 2 m courses |
| Wind girder | walkway ring 1.07–1.2 m below the top (API 650 5.9.6, secondhand); photo about 1.2–1.4 m | **1.2 m below the top** (the sheet drew 1.5 m) |
| Tank stair | straight flights with landings wrapping the shell at 40–45°, ending on the girder walkway, then a short flight to the platform | one stair wound round the shell at about 40°, built as a continuous helix: on the block grid it reads like the photo's flights |
| Gauger's platform | about 1.5 × 2.5 m at the rim, carries the ladder pivot [unc] | 2 m platform, as drawn |
| Rolling ladder | length about H, so it reaches the roof landed on its legs; nearly flat with the roof high; runway about as long as the ladder | **length = fill height H**; runway on the deck of the same length |
| Pontoon | annulus 8–12 % of D [unc]; rim gap about 0.2 m with a seal | 0.1 D, as drawn |
| Roof legs | operating 1.0–1.2 m, maintenance 1.8–2.0 m | 1.8 m, as drawn |
| Tank nozzles | 24–36 in, centres 0.75–1 m above the floor; ringwall 0.3–0.5 m [unc] | centres on the lowest cell row (0.5 m), as drawn |
| Colour | white shell; grey or rust roof deck | paint channels; roof in the structure colour |
| Sphere legs | πD/5 to πD/4.5 legs (18 m sphere on 11 columns, about 4.9 m apart); leg diameter 0.05–0.06 D; leg tops about 0.05 D above the equator; concrete fireproofing up to about 0.3 D on some | even count ≈ πD/4.5 (at least 4); **leg width 0.05 D**; tops 1 m above the equator; fireproofing left out |
| Clearance | 0.07–0.1 D (1.2–2 m) | 2–8 m, default 2 m |
| Bracing | one tier of crossed rods per bay, below the equator to near the base | as drawn |
| Sphere stair | (a) from grade, following the shell over the top (Woodhead 52082578052, MiRO); (b) trestle stair to an equator landing, then a curved flight (AMOCO 03) | **(a), approved**: wound round outside the legs, then over the upper hemisphere |
| Crown | railing ring 0.3–0.4 D across; two relief valves with vertical stacks 2–3 m tall; manway 20–24 in | ring platform 0.36 D; **two valves with 2 m vertical stacks**; manway |
| Bottom nozzle | DN 150–300 at the pole, valve at the shell, elbow to a line leaving between two legs | N2 as drawn; it leaves to the front, between the two front legs |

The bold entries refine the approved sheets. They are drawing details only, with no physics or port change.

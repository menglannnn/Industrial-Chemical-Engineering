# Chunk loading of the fluid network and custom machines: review

2026-10-26, at `2fbb80f2` (`main`, 0.12.3), worktree `chunk-loading-behavior-1796a8`. Code read against the NeoForge 21.1.219 sources (`build/moddev/artifacts/neoforge-21.1.219-sources.jar`). Sections 1 to 3: the review (no code changed). Section 4: re-check at `9eeeb1f4` and fixes (`7147d431`, release prep `600549d9`). Section 5: gates.

## 1. Expected behaviour

- A plant runs whether or not a player is near it. Pumps keep pumping, tanks keep filling and mass and energy keep balancing in unloaded chunks, on online ticks only. Nothing needs a chunk ticket.
- Only the world-facing side follows chunks: blocks, collision (floating roofs), block-entity views, GUIs and world-space displays. After a reload they show the state the plant has reached, at the next bucket (100 ticks, about 5 s) at the latest, and they load no further chunks.

## 2. What the code does

As expected:

- Islands, ledger, scheduler and saved data never read a chunk (`PhysicalFluidTopology`, `IslandCoordinator`), and no ticket is ever taken. An island across loaded and unloaded chunks solves as one.
- `ChunkEvent.Load` queues a `TickTask` for `FluidWorldAuthority.loadedChunk`. It rebinds pipes and vessels, registers missing ones (after a format reset) and marks the chunk's devices. `onLoad` and `onChunkUnloaded` mark and unmark (`deviceLoaded`, `deviceUnloaded`). Publications mark only loaded devices. An unload drops the cached menu, glide, sight-glass and mover data, so a GUI opened just after a reload waits for its bucket, as the presentation rule says.
- Multi-chunk equipment: `restoreFluidBinding` registers only when every chunk of the box is loaded. `refreshLater` waits until the whole region is block-ticking. Nozzle and face reads check `isLoaded`. Recoveries and gauge commits wait for a loaded chunk.

Defects:

- **D1 (high, performance, not measured): a pipe's load loads neighbouring chunks, in a chain.** `FluidDeviceBlockEntity.onLoad` → `refreshGeometry(…, true)` → `FluidDeviceBlock.connectedState` (`FluidDeviceBlock.java:89`) reads `level.getBlockState` of all six neighbours with no `isLoaded` check. On a server level, that call loads a missing chunk on the main thread: `Level.getBlockState` → `ServerChunkCache.getChunk(…, FULL, true)` adds an `UNKNOWN` ticket (1 tick) and blocks until the chunk is loaded. Each pipe on the edge of a border chunk (FULL, ticket level 33) loads the chunk beyond it. That chunk's pipes run `onLoad` on the next tick and load the next chunk. The chain goes on through every chunk that has a pipe on its far edge, one blocking load per chunk per tick. It runs again each time the border chunk loads again, for example while a player walks. `computeFaces` (line 150) already checks `isLoaded`; `connectedState` does not. The `server.execute` in `onLoad` does not defer the read: on the server thread, `BlockableEventLoop.execute` runs the task at once.
- **D2 (medium on small tanks): a floating roof jumps after a reload.** An unload drops the tank's glide (`deviceUnloaded` → `publishedGlides.remove`, `FloatingRoofs.remove`). A reload puts the roof at the node cell's saved `RoofLevel`, which is the level at the unload. The island kept running, so at the first bucket `TankDisplay.Glide.next(null, …)` moves the roof to the current level at once. The jump is Δh = Q·t/A. Example, a 0.1 m line at 2 m/s (57 m³/h) for 30 min: Δh = 2.3 m in a 4 m tank, 0.36 m in a 10 m tank, 0.02 m in a 40 m tank. Entities that load with the chunk stand on the old roof. If the roof falls, they fall Δh. If the roof rises more than `LIFT` (0.6 m), they end up inside the roof's collision, because `carry` lifts only entities whose feet are within 0.6 m below the surface.
- **D3 (low): a column solve continues after its calculator unloads.** `ColumnCalculatorV3BlockEntity` has no unload hook, and by design it does not save its active operation. A job that runs when the chunk unloads keeps its owner slot. The reloaded calculator shows "ready to calculate", but a new Calculate is refused with `OWNER_BUSY` until the old job ends (deadline 45 s). The old job's result is then dropped (`STALE_TARGET` or `STALE_RESULT`).
- **D4 (low): tank watches stay after an unload.** `deviceUnloaded` ends sight-glass and mover watches, but not `tankWatchers`. The entries stay until the client unwatches or logs out (at most `MAXIMUM_WATCHED` per player). No wrong data is sent.
- Aside (outside this review's scope, not checked in game): `FluidDeviceBlock.updateShape` uses the same immediate `execute`. Its `refreshFacesAndSync` therefore reads the connections from before the update. A later topology commit's redraw normally corrects the faces.

## 3. Proposals for the owner

- **P1 (D1).** In `connectedState`, a face whose neighbour chunk is not loaded keeps its saved connection bit. This is the rule `computeFaces` already uses, and it is what `PhysicalFluidTopology` assumes ("static fit, independent of whether the neighbouring chunk is loaded"). When the neighbour chunk loads, its own `onLoad` refresh updates both sides. Also remove the `server.execute` wrapper in `onLoad`, because it changes nothing. The fix deletes nothing and stops the chain. Check: one dev-server run (server log only, no client) that counts `ChunkEvent.Load` beyond view distance along a 20-chunk pipe line, before and after the fix.
- **P2 (D2), options.** A (recommended): when a tank has no previous glide, its first glide starts at the level where the roof stands (the saved level) and reaches the new level over one bucket. The existing `carry` then lifts riders on a rising roof (2.3 m over 100 ticks is 0.023 m per tick, well inside `LIFT`), and riders follow a falling roof. This is one change in `publishTank` or `Glide.next`, with no new state. B: keep the jump and move the entities on the roof by Δh at the first bucket. C: accept the jump.
- **P3 (D3), options.** A (recommended): `onChunkUnloaded` and `setRemoved` cancel the calculator's job (`ProcessSolveServices.cancelRequest`). This frees the worker and the owner slot. B: save the operation, so that a reloaded calculator takes the result. This reverses the decision not to save operations.
- **P4 (D4).** `deviceUnloaded` also calls `tankWatchers.remove(id)`, as it already does for sight glasses and movers.

## 4. Re-check at `9eeeb1f4` (0.13.1) and the fixes

- Owner, 2026-10-26: "Code on main updated, review, assess, implement". The branch was fast-forwarded to local `main` `9eeeb1f4` (0.13.0 backward compatibility, 0.13.1 accessory store). None of the files behind D1 to D4 changed, so all four stand. The new load paths (`removeRetiredLater` / `removeRetired`, `layoutIncomplete`) wait, as `refreshLater` does, until their whole region is block-ticking. They load no chunk beyond it, except a walk over interior cells (`retiredReached`, `shellReached`, up to `FIND_STEPS` = 42 blocks) that starts at another vessel's interior and runs past the region. That is rare and happens once per structure, so it is left as is.
- P1: `connectedState` skips a face whose neighbour chunk is not loaded (`level.hasChunkAt`). The inline `server.execute` in `FluidDeviceBlockEntity.onLoad` is removed and `refreshGeometry` is called directly, with the same order and the same reads. Residual: if a loading pipe's registration differs from its block state, `refreshGeometry` still sets the block with `UPDATE_ALL`, and vanilla neighbour updates then read all six neighbours. With P1 such a difference comes only from a loaded neighbour or from a fitting edit, not from an unloaded chunk.
- P2, option A: `Glide.next(old, standing, display, tick)` starts at `standing` when there is no glide since the load. `FloatingRoofs.standing` gives the roof's level, which is its glide's level or the saved level, or NaN if no tank is loaded (NaN means the first display stands at once, as before). `present` calls `publishTank` before `acceptFluidView`, so the saved level is read before the new view overwrites it.
- P3, option A: `ColumnCalculatorV3BlockEntity.setRemoved` cancels the active operation through `ProcessSolveServices.cancelRequest`. A chunk unload calls `setRemoved` after `onChunkUnloaded`, so the one hook covers both unloading and breaking.
- P4: `deviceUnloaded` calls `tankWatchers.remove(id)`.
- Gates: `FloatingRoofsTest.aReloadedRoofGlidesFromTheLevelItsNodeCellSaved` (unit test), and `ChunkLoadGameTests` (2 fluid GameTests). The first GameTest runs `connectedState` on a stub level whose east chunk is not loaded. The second removes a calculator in the tick its solve was admitted, then checks that the solve was already cancelled.
- Test leak found on the way: `EquipmentCompatGameTests.aBlockedRefreshTriesAgainAtTheNextLoad` (0.13.0) left its drum registered. GameTest areas are not cleared between batches (`StructureGridSpawner(…, false)`), and slots keep advancing. On `main` the next test's clearing margin (−2/+3 blocks) happened to reach the drum and dismantle it. The two new tests moved the drum's test to the end of a row, so nothing removed it, and `FluidHarnessGameTests`' check that no registration is left failed. Fix: that test now removes its drum.

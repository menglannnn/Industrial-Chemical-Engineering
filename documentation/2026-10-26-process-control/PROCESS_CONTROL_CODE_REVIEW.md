## Physics defects
2. **Boot drums count the boot's water column twice** (`science/fluid/network/VesselGeometry.java:78`, `PassiveStepSolver.portHead` :1544). Expected: the vessel node stands at the body-bottom cell (`ShapeRasterizer.fluidCell`), and its pipes take elevations from that node, so a port's head is the liquid above the body bottom. The boot column below is the "line below the nozzle", which the pipe's elevation drop already carries. Code: `head()` integrates from the boot bottom (`-bootDepth`). Every liquid port of a boot drum (BOOT_WATER, BOOT_HC) gains rho_w·g·bootDepth: ≥ 5.6 kPa on a 2 m drum (boot depth ≥ 0.57 m), about 10 kPa per metre of boot. The old prism law had no such term. Proposal: integrate the head from z = 0 (the body bottom) for every port. CONFIRMED. No test covers it: the head test uses a sphere without a boot.
3. **Round vessels drop the solids' mass from the hydrostatic head** (:1544). Expected: the slurry head is rho_slurry·g·h. Code: it uses `phaseVolume` (which includes the solids' volume share) with `phaseMass` (fluid only). Example: 20 vol% sand (2500 kg/m³) in water gives 800 kg/m³ instead of 1300 kg/m³, so the head is 38 % low. A vessel holding only solids gets zero head. The prism law used `liquidMass`, which includes solids. Proposal: one phase column mass, `phaseMass + share·solidMass`, consistent with `phaseVolume`. CONFIRMED.
4. **The mixed-liquid stream at vanishing flow is 50/50 whatever the amounts** (`PhaseDraw.leading` :52, `endDensity` :1556, `mixedLiquidStream`, `InletPhase`). Expected: an outlet from 1 m³ of water holding an 8.7e-24 m³ HC film carries water. The equal-volume rule exhausts the film after 1e-24 m³ of draw. Code: `holdsPhase` is a strict `> 0`. Once any trace appears, the outlet's vanishing-flow density, viscosity, velocity limit and composition jump to an equal-volume HC/water mix: about 50 vol% HC in the seeded junction stream, and a viscosity jump of up to tenfold. `leading()` returns -1 for "both liquids", which used to mean "no phase". Two callers do not check for it: `NetworkReduction.resolutionFlow` (bulk viscosity, gas included) and `SolidEventIntegrator.mobility` (MIXED outlet, donor mass with gas). Option A (recommended): build the vanishing-flow stream with `fillMixed` over the connection volume, as `columnDensity` already does, and replace the -1 sentinel with an explicit mixed result. It is continuous in the trace amount, keeps the approved co-draw rule, needs no cutoff and discards nothing. Option B (reopens the owner's rule): heavy liquid first at ordinary bottoms. That is real stratification, the pre-branch law. CONFIRMED (code). It is NOT the cause of seed 236: see Fixes.

## Runtime and cost
5. **Flowmeters lose their local tap after a reload, and a new pipe flowmeter has none until the next recompile** (`runtime/fluid/FluidWorldAuthority.java:476`). `flowmeter()` calls `edit()` with the unchanged device and spec, and `edit()` returns null for that case (:248). No recompile happens. The checkpoint codec does not write observation points. Until some other topology event, the actual-volume reading falls back to donor density: about 2x wrong on a gas line where the tap is at half the upstream pressure. Proposal: re-attach the compiled observation points to the restored islands in `PhysicalRegistry.load`. Queue an observation-only island update on install and removal. Observation points stay outside `Pipe.Identity`. CONFIRMED.
6. **An island with a flow-control valve never rests** (`NetworkReduction.java:164`). Expected: a flow valve only throttles, so the island is at rest when the passive drive across the valve is within the rest resolution. Code: `atRest` returns false whenever a configured flow valve is present, even an empty or closed one. Proposal: treat a flow valve as a one-way passive drive (`drives[i][1] = -inf`). CONFIRMED. Cost impact PLAUSIBLE.
7. **Dependent-row detection covers mass-flow rows only** (`ControlConstraints.java:23`). A FLOW module installs VOLUME_FLOW, so the common case, two flowmeter valves in series on a liquid line, is not detected. That case is structurally dependent (incompressible). No test covers it: `twoFlowValvesInSeries…` is mass-only. Proposal: include volume rows when the drawn stream is liquid. Or, as a simplification for the owner to consider: drop VALVE_CONFLICT and let the existing VALVE_OPEN transition decide, so the lower setpoint governs and the other valve goes fully open, which is what happens in a real plant. PLAUSIBLE.
8. **`ControlConstraints.dependent` runs a dense elimination on every `solve()`** (:344) and in every valve's presentation. For J junction rows over E pipes this is O(J·E²) time and O(E²) memory: E = 300 is about 2.7e7 flops and 0.7 MB per call. The campaign worlds (≤ 20 blocks) cannot show this. Proposal: cache by structure (pipe identities and junction flags), for example beside `structures`. CONFIRMED (code); cost PLAUSIBLE.

## Minor
9. The flowmeter flash in `observe()` (:1497) catches only IllegalArgumentException. Another failure, for example `PhaseLayout.Vanished` or a nonconvergence, would escape from an accepted step, and a display-only sensor would then reject it. Catch every RuntimeException except the checkpoint's cancellation. PLAUSIBLE.
10. The boot drum's body level reads 0 m when empty, then jumps to -bootDepth at the first drop (`Sensor.java:76`, from the `v==0` special case and the boot-bottom origin of `heightAt`). TankDisplay's water level also goes negative. CONFIRMED.
11. A BOOT_HC port turns off the block Jacobian for its whole island (`:4193`, `hasWeirs → -1`, colored sweep). The weir row's `clamp` is not held to the base branch inside `linearising`. CONFIRMED (code); cost unmeasured.
12. A sensor commit waits up to 100 ticks, then drops the operation silently if the held stack object changed. The valve GUI reads its mode by parsing the status string. Both are usability or fragility notes only.

## Fixes (2026-10-27, branch `claude/process-control-fixes` off `8523595a`, commit `fa62fd81`)
- 2: `VesselGeometry.head` integrates from z = 0; a boot's column below counts only in the line. Test `bootHeadStandsOverTheBodyBottom`.
- 3: the round-vessel head adds each phase's solid share to its mass (`phaseMass + share·solidMass`); a solids-only vessel weighs its solids. Test `aSlurryHeadWeighsItsSolids`.
- 4, option A: `PhaseDraw.mixedShares` fills the connection volume equal-volume until the smaller liquid runs out, then the rest from the larger. Density, viscosity, solid share, moles, mass and enthalpy of the end stream take these shares. `resolutionFlow` and `SolidEventIntegrator.mobility` use the mixed result, not the -1 sentinel. A 7e-20 mol HC film on 1 m³ of water now draws water; 0.5/0.5 is reached only when each liquid fills half the line. Test `aTraceFilmDoesNotTurnTheBottomStreamHalfOil`.

## The mechanism (form A): the island's quasi-steady balance is the start of every solve without a carried point, and of the mint

**Physics.** A junction holds `m_J` = 0.05 s of its largest cap flow (11 g of air or 3.9 kg of LNG behind a 50 mm line) and its pressure settles within the acoustic time of its lines, 1 to 3 ms, against a 50 ms to 5 s step. So a step's junctions start from the network's quasi-steady solution on the vessels' current states: every junction's net inflow zero under every connection's own law (the loss inverted on the drive in the donor's column, saturated at the donor's cap, nothing in a refused direction; a pump's prescribed flow or its rise; a filter's cake law), the vessels, generators and voids held. Each line's law is monotone in its drive, so the balance is the minimum of a convex co-content and unique up to the flat ranges of choked lines; a junction's pressure never leaves the hull of what its neighbours can drive (held ends, columns, rises). A flashing junction's density is a function of its own pressure (PH at its enthalpy): LNG let down from 5.6 MPa is vapour at 1 atm (10 kg/m3, a 50 mm cap of 2 kg/s) and liquid at its bubble pressure (400 kg/m3, 77 kg/s), so the balance carries that function (a 5-point table in ln P per two-phase junction) or it oscillates between the two. In numbers: cold 15's junction minted at 412 kPa under a 2.75 MPa generator with its supply line at 13 kg/s moves to 2.7 MPa with the line at the 2.1 kg/s its vents pass; bundled 988's three runs get their own law flows (0.034 / 0.037 kg/s and the bottleneck's) instead of one contracted 0.052 kg/s past every cap, and the step converges in 56 Newton iterations where the baseline held after 690; cold 19's rearranged all-choked network starts balanced and converges where the baseline stalls at |r| 961. Pump modes are decided on the balance: a target whose demanded head exceeds the rise goes to the head limit; a suction junction below the bubble pressure of the liquid it draws (the K6 floor, the water curve) is anchored there and the pump passes what arrives (the K6 gate `anLngSupplierAtItsBubblePointHoldsItsPumpOnTheSuctionLimit` passes from the start). The balance is a starting guess only: no inventory moves, no converged root changes.

**What it is in code** (`PassiveStepSolver.BalanceNet`, `balanceStart`, `balanceStarts`, 330 code lines in the patch): D12's one-way estimate (`OneWay`, a damped Newton on a banded Jacobian plus a Gauss-Seidel sweep kept only when it improves) promoted from a rate-only cold special case to the start, with every mode's law, the density tables, the upwind-mixture rounds (2) at the balanced flows, the one-way closures read off the balanced drives, and the pump-mode decisions. It runs when no carried point exists (a new structure, a mint, a rate solve on other states) and whenever no step of the structure has been accepted; the warm carry of a continued structure is unchanged.

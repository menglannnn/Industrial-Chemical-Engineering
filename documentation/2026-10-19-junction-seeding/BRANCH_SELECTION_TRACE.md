## Bundled 4
**World** (pair-minimal, 10 blocks, uncertified on `33455d4c` and STEADY on `32bf3fa4` at 40 slices): KEEP `1@179,2@184,4@227,5@234,6@281,10@415,11@605,14@1404,16@1498,18@1853`. An N2 header: gen[N2 285 K 415 kPa] → J12 → J14 → void, all 50 mm. A crude source teed in through 20 mm: gen[crude 369 K 261 kPa] → J10 → J1 → valve[200 kPa] → J14. The last block closes a 20 mm run J10–J12. There is no vessel, and the join mints every junction anew.
**Physics: one steady state.** At the 100 m/s cap the vent passes 0.196 m³/s. To carry what the generator delivers, the vent junction needs nearly the generator's density. So the header sits at 400–409 kPa and vents 0.933 kg/s (ρ 4.75 kg/m³). The crude source is 143 kPa below the tee, so crude cannot enter. Flow into a generator is refused (a non-return valve), so the crude line closes. The dead legs carry 0.080 kg/s of N2 through the open valve. A crude-flowing state cannot exist: below 261 kPa the generator line's capped N2 alone (0.967 kg/s) needs ≥ 0.33 m³/s at the vent, so the header must rise above the crude's pressure, and crude only adds volume. History cannot select a branch: there is no stock, and the boundaries alone fix the state. A real plant does the same.

**`33455d4c`.**

That is a true root of the frozen equations, not of the island: its junctions book 0.52 kg/s of N2 accumulation, venting 1.33 kg/s of hydrocarbon for 0.81 kg/s entering.
4. The step starts at that root and is within 2 % of the steady state after 6 s. The balance's fixed point starts from an arbitrary placeholder, is cut at two rounds, and the cold rate seed freezes the cut iterate into the junctions. The model's equations are right there: the step relaxes to the physical state.

## 1. Cold 778: E4 at tick 1185 (7 blocks)
- **Physics, steady state.** A 1 m³ tank is liquid-full of LNG at 164.9 K and 4.156 MPa, with 19.25 kg of CO2 crystal. A CO2 header (312 K, 4.43 MPa) blows through the junction under the tank to air at 2.39 kg/s, the 100 m/s cap. The LNG generator (3.38 MPa) is below the tank. The steady state is rest for the tank, with the vent at its cap through a junction at 307 to 309 K that holds no solid.
- **The transient root.** If tank liquid drains into that junction, as the step's large-step root has it (0.24 to 0.47 kg/s), its crystal share (5.3 %, about 0.011 kg/s) arrives 90 K above the CO2 melting point at 4.1 MPa (about 217 K). It melts at once. Taking it to gas at 307 K costs about 0.7 MJ/kg, so 7.6 kW, which cools the 2.39 kg/s CO2 stream by about 2 K. Nothing solid stays.
- **What the code did.** Node 3, the junction under the tank, fails on two rows: solid moment 2 and temperature. The residual is exactly proportional to dt (0.013 at 0.107 s, 1.93e-7 at 1.6e-6 s). The reconstruction books a constant 0.011 kg/s crystal rate into the junction, and the Newton point does not state that rate.
- **Proposition.** Crystal above its melting point melts on arrival in a junction. This is stated once, in the solid stage that both the Newton's junction rows and `ConservativeTransport` read. A junction gives no solid support above the crystal's melting temperature. The crystal it receives is booked as its fluid component, with the crystal's enthalpy, so the fusion is charged to the junction.
- **Risk.** It amends D23 ("crystal mass moves only in a vessel's refresh") for junctions. References in which crystal crosses a warm junction may move. Gate: 778's 7-block world keeps up and goes back to rest. Rule-off proof: that one test.

## 2. Cold 743: E5 at tick 11358 (14 blocks)
- **Physics, steady state.** A 1 m³ tank holds two-phase LNG (4.96 MPa, 200.7 K, 36.7 kg of CO2 crystal). Junction 0 joins the tank (50 mm, plugged), a 10 mm vent to air (plugged) and an LNG supply line (120 K, 485 kPa). With both plugs held, the steady state is rest: the inflow decays from 0.75 kg/s at 5058 to 0.0077 kg/s at 11258. Once the vent opens, the real plant blows junction 0 down in seconds. Below 485 kPa the LNG supply then flows through it to air at about 0.5 kg/s (about 15 m/s in 10 mm), which is a flowing steady state, not a hold.
- **What the code did.** At 11258 the vent plug lifts while the tank side stays plugged. Junction 0 (2.2 kg) blows down. The iterate takes it to 0.73 MPa with the supply feeding 0.47 kg/s, and every step size stalls. The failing rows are the momentum rows of pipes 1 and 2 and junction 0's temperature row. The supply line was open at m19 and was plugged at zero velocity on `5b49f940`. Keep it open, because that is physical.
- **Proposition.**
  - (a) Trace why the plug lifts at 11258 and not before. This is code-free and comes first. t54's suspect: the still decision flips on the reopened plugged pair as the inflow decays (D33 reopens every closure at an interval start, and a still pipe keeps the reopened state).
  - (b) A still pipe keeps its state with its plug: the state it ended the last interval in, not the reopened one. A plug lifts only on a real interval-start drive.
  - (c) A junction that crosses a supply's pressure is sized as a start transient: substeps from the interval start, as after a topology change.
- **Trace (t56, 2026-10-20, on `a08c7134`, no code change; probes in `tools/crystal-junction-holds/probes/`).** The 14-block world holds E5 at 11358 (committed 11258) as the fuzz does. t54's suspect is not the cause: no plug is carried, and the supply line plays no part until the balance moves junction 0.
  - **Every interval** from about 9000 on runs as three segments. D33 reopens both plugs. The interval-start rate solve passes 1.70 kg/s from the tank through junction 0 to the vent; the tank line reads 1.3681 m/s against a deposition velocity 1e-8 above it, so the check allows it. Inside the first steps it falls below and closes (DEPOSITION, at t = 0.058 s at 10658, shrinking ×0.68 per interval to 0.0042 s at 11358). The next segment's first check closes the vent (0.019 m/s against 0.247 m/s). The third segment runs plugged.
  - **The third segment's start** is a cold rate seed, a rate solve from the island's balance. Junction 0 (2.25 kg, liquid-full at 4.9557 MPa, 0.30 kg of crystal) is then sealed: both plugs closed, and the still LNG line open to a 485 kPa generator it stands 4.5 MPa above, which refuses a reverse flow. Its net inflow is exactly zero at every pressure above 485 kPa.
  - **Why 11258 and not before.** In every interval up to 11258 the balance's Newton converges, and junction 0 keeps its pressure; the start closure then closes the supply line, and the segment runs. At 11358 the CO2 make-up line (junctions 6 and 7, 0.0077 kg/s and decaying) leaves the Newton's step not descending (residual 5.9e-4 against 9.3e-5). The balance falls back to its Gauss-Seidel sweep, which bisects every unknown junction on the sign of its net inflow. For junction 0, whose inflow was already zero, that bisection lands on the edge of the flat range: exactly 485 157.7 Pa. The merit improves through junctions 6 and 7, so the sweep is kept.
  - Junction 0 is then seeded as vapour at 485 kPa and 94 to 200 K. The rate seed fails (temperature row, 0.184), the step starts from the same balance, and every step size stalls on the same row. t54's failing iterate (vent open, junction 0 at 0.73 MPa) is the second segment's steps; the hold itself is the third segment.
- **Physics.** A fitting sealed between two plugs and a one-way supply it stands above holds its fluid and its pressure: nothing flows in or out. The balance has no reason to move it, since its row is already zero.
- **Proposition (d), from the trace; replaces (b) and (c) for 743.** The balance's Gauss-Seidel sweep leaves a sealed junction at its pressure: one none of whose open connections carries anything there (each refused the way its drive points, or without a drive). A first form, "a junction within the balance tolerance keeps its pressure", broke the static-column gate (seed 322: a near-balanced junction with live flows no longer re-bisected, the balance stayed unconverged, wall deadline), so the rule is the sealed fitting only. This lives in the balance (`PassiveStepSolver.BalanceNet.sweep`), the stage that moved the junction. (b) does not reach 743: the plugs are real-flow closures, not roundoff-still pipes. (c) is not needed: the blowdown never starts in the trajectory. The tank line's flicker at its deposition threshold (1e-8) is a separate cost (three segments per interval), not the hold.
